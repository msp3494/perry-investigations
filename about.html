Jeffrey E. Perry

Massachusetts Licensed Private Investigator, senior investigative executive, and consultant with more than 25 years of public-service and investigative experience.

Executive Biography

Jeffrey E. Perry concluded a distinguished career with the Massachusetts State Police in July 2026 after decades of service to the Commonwealth, retiring at the rank of Captain within the Division of Investigative Services.

As a Captain, Perry provided executive oversight of geographically dispersed investigative units and previously commanded the Norfolk State Police Detective Unit, the Cyber Crime Unit and Massachusetts Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force, and the Technical Services Unit.

His career includes complex multi-jurisdictional investigations involving homicide, narcotics, violent and property crime, financial crime, child exploitation, cybercrime, and digital evidence, as well as extensive experience interviewing witnesses and suspects, reviewing electronically stored information, developing investigative findings, and presenting technical evidence in court.

Perry’s background also includes physical-security technology, threat and risk assessment, workplace violence, critical-incident response, and executive board service with the FBI New England Regional Computer Forensics Laboratory and the Massachusetts Digital Evidence Consortium.

Today, he leads Perry Advisory & Investigations LLC, providing investigative, intelligence, and security consulting services to attorneys, law firms, private clients, and public-sector organizations.
