Discreet Massachusetts private investigative support for individuals and families facing sensitive matters where reliable information, sound judgment, and careful fact development matter.

When Independent Fact-Finding Can Help

Clear answers for sensitive personal matters

Private clients often contact Perry Advisory & Investigations when a situation is important, difficult to resolve independently, or requires a professional and objective approach.

Each matter begins with a confidential discussion to understand the objective, determine whether an investigation is appropriate, and define a lawful and proportionate scope.

  • Locating or reconnecting with an individual
  • Background and due-diligence questions before consequential decisions
  • Online or digital matters requiring careful fact development
  • Concerning behavior, threats, or personal-safety concerns
  • Complex personal or family matters requiring independent fact-finding
  • Coordination with legal counsel when a matter has legal implications

A Careful Fit Assessment

Not every matter requires a private investigation

The initial consultation is also an opportunity to determine whether Perry Advisory & Investigations is the right fit.

Some matters may be better handled by an attorney, law-enforcement agency, mental-health professional, financial institution, or another specialist. When appropriate, the firm can coordinate with a client’s legal counsel so investigative work supports the broader strategy without duplicating legal advice.
